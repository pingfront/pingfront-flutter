## 1.0.0

- First release: the native pingfront iOS and Android SDKs behind one typed Dart API (Pigeon), with the native chat.
- The chat presented over your Flutter view (`openChat`) or embedded in your layout (`PingFrontChatView`).
- Identity (`identify`, `identifyWithToken`), traits, entitlements, offers, moments, bug reports, push, chat links
  (`handleUrl`), saved links into your screens (`registerScreens`, `onScreen`) and the self-check.
- `PingFront.conversation`, the thread and its actions for a chat UI of your own.
- Language, native log lines, the resolved look and team actions.
- Vendor adapters through the add-on packages `pingfront_revenuecat`, `pingfront_superwall` and `pingfront_adapty`.
